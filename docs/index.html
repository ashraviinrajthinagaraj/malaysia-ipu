How this works

Readings: Department of Environment Malaysia, APIMS. History: OpenDOSM. Map: OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles, © OpenStreetMap contributors; map engine MapLibre GL JS. Place search: Nominatim (OpenStreetMap). Forecast estimate: CAMS (Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service) via Open-Meteo. Fire hotspots: NASA FIRMS. Official closure announcements: Bernama reports. District boundaries: data.gov.my. Wind: Open-Meteo.

An independent dashboard, not an official government service. School-status predictions are not closure announcements.
